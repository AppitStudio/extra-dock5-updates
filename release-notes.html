ExtraDock 5.0.3 – Every Dock, One Click Away 🎛️

A stabilization release: floating docks hold their position, Finder clicks behave like Finder clicks, launcher apps stop bouncing, and every dock you’ve configured is now reachable straight from the menu bar.

✨ What’s New

  • Every Dock in the Menu Bar: The ExtraDock status icon now lists each configured dock as its own submenu — Show/Hide, Enabled, Position Mode, Size and Open in Manager…, with New Dock right at the top. The same list appears in the ExtraDock app menu.
  • Lock Position Is Back: Floating docks get ExtraDock 4’s Lock Position toggle right in their menu, so a dock stays exactly where you put it.
  • Export Dock Diagnostics: The Manager’s dock menu and Settings → Diagnostics can now write a readable report of a dock’s settings and live state, or all docks at once. App paths, custom names, folder and network locations are redacted first, so the file is safe to send along with a support email.
  • A Bigger Collapse Button: The collapse button now fills the largest square its slot allows (33 pt → 48 pt at default settings), and custom text accepts up to five characters instead of three. Labels no longer shrink or clip as the dock opens and closes.

🐞 Fixed

  • Floating docks stay put: Moving the pointer near a dock, or simply leaving one idle next to the macOS Dock, could make it drift, shake or hop between two positions. Only a real drag moves a dock now.
  • Magnification grows straight out: On a vertical floating dock, magnified icons slid along the bar instead of growing in place. They now expand symmetrically, and their click target matches what you see.
  • Finder clicks behave like Finder clicks: Finder now honors your dock’s frontmost action — None, Minimize, Hide or Cycle — instead of always jumping to a folder. Minimizing and clicking again restores that window rather than opening a duplicate, and alternating between the macOS Dock and ExtraDock no longer produces dead clicks. Both Finder menus gained the standard New Finder Window command.
  • Launcher apps stop bouncing: Apps, Screenshot and similar system launchers open their window and quit within milliseconds, which left their icon bouncing for 20–30 seconds with clicks ignored. The icon now settles the moment the app hands off.
  • Reserve Dock Space respects keyboard and window-manager moves: Snapping a window to a half with a shortcut or Raycast now settles inside your reserved space, even with Active on Drag turned off.
  • The Screens tab follows the dock you picked: Switching docks in the sidebar while Screens was open left assignments pointing at the previous dock, making per-dock screen settings look universal. Toggles now land on the dock you’re actually editing.
  • Old ExtraDock 4 dock copies import cleanly: Copies made before March 2026 reused item IDs and failed on import. They’re now repaired automatically, preserving V4 order and content.

🚀 What’s Next?

Nearly every fix in this release started as a tester report or a screen recording — keep them coming, and they go straight to the top of the list.
