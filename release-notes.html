ExtraDock 5.0.1 – Polish, Custom Icons & DockFlow! 🎨🔗

✨ What's New

  • DockFlow Integration: ExtraDock now publishes your docks to DockFlow so the two apps can work together — and the connection stays rock-solid even when DockFlow applies a preset that restarts the macOS Dock!
  • App-Wide Running Indicator Toggle: A new master switch in Settings → Behavior lets you show or hide the running-app dots across every dock at once — individual docks keep their own opt-out too!
  • Custom Indicator Color: Pick one color for running indicators across all your docks, or leave it on Default to keep each dock's accent color with the adaptive light/dark palette!
  • Custom Finder Icons: The Finder widget can now wear artwork of your choosing, with a one-click Use Default to put it back!
  • Clock Type Size Control: A new Type Size control on the Clock widget lets you dial the text size exactly where you want it!

⚡ Improvements

  • Running Dots, the Native Way: Running indicators now sit just outside the icon with native-Dock spacing — slightly larger, never overlapping your artwork, and always legible in both light and dark appearance!
  • Respects Your Launch Animation Setting: Turn off Animate opening applications in macOS and ExtraDock skips the launch bounce too — Reduce Motion still wins!
  • Gesture Menus Tell the Truth: When click or scroll gestures are off app-wide, item menus now show that clearly and offer Enable Click Gestures / Enable Scroll Gestures right where you need them!
  • Deeper Integration Diagnostics: ExtraDock now logs Dock restarts, dock repairs, and every DockFlow request and response, making integration issues much easier to trace!

🐛 Bug Fixes

  • Docks Survive a Dock Restart: When macOS relaunches its own Dock, your ExtraDock docks repair themselves instead of disappearing — hidden docks stay hidden, and show/hide is only confirmed once the window actually reaches the requested state!
  • Gauge Text Colors Are Back: CPU, GPU and Memory widgets get their Automatic / White / Black / Custom text modes again — including colors carried over from ExtraDock 4!
  • Clock No Longer Shrinks After Import: Imported clocks keep their full two-cell width!
  • Flexible Spacer Width Restored: Custom width is back to the full 2–3000 point range, and V4 spacer sizes import correctly!
  • Running Apps Gestures Stick: Per-app click and scroll overrides now save durably and survive a restart!
  • V4 Import & Multi-Display Fixes: Hidden docks import correctly again, and docks land on the display you expect!
  • Smooth Multi-Display Dragging: Dragging a dock between displays no longer shakes or jumps at the screen seam, with steadier edge and corner snapping!
  • App Stack Custom Icons Are Back: Full ExtraDock 4 parity — pick your own artwork, revert to default, and imported V4 icons come along for the ride!
  • No More Endless Bouncing: Launcher-style apps like Apps and Screenshot can no longer leave an icon bouncing and unclickable — a 20-second safety net always ends it!
  • Chrome Web Apps Restore Properly: Un-minimizing a Chrome-installed web app no longer makes it flash back and disappear!
  • Microsoft Teams Stays Alive: Clicking Teams in your dock no longer risks terminating it — it now uses safe public activation!
  • Running Indicators No Longer Clip: The running dot stays fully visible at Small, Medium, and Large on every edge and floating orientation!
  • Dark Mode Icons Refresh: App artwork updates the moment you switch appearance — no more stale light-mode icons!
  • Widgets Fill Their Cells Again: Launcher and static widgets are back to full size — no more shrunken artwork at Medium and Large!
  • No More Double Instances: Relaunching ExtraDock no longer leaves two copies running at once!
  • Reliable Diagnostics Export: Exporting logs no longer fails with a cryptic error — it retries, verifies the file, and reports real problems clearly!

🚀 What's Next?

We're always working on making ExtraDock more powerful and intuitive! More features and improvements are on the way based on your feedback!
