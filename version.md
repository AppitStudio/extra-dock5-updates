VERSION: 5.2.6
DETAILS:

new: Dock tabs: A dock can now hold several sets of items, each with its own tab. Switch tabs from the strip next to the dock, and in Manager → Items choose whether the tabs are Always Visible or appear On Hover. Tabs show just their name by default; turn on "Tab Background" for the filled look
new: GIF widget: A free widget that plays a GIF, animated PNG or WebP from your Mac, at the same size as the app icons beside it. A click can grow it out of the dock, open an app or folder, play a sound or open a link. It keeps its own copy of the file, and picks up your changes when you edit or replace it. ExtraDock 4 GIF widgets carry over when you import
new: Bring to this screen: A new click gesture (the new Shift-click default) moves an app's window to the screen of the dock you clicked. Manager → Behavior → Clicking can also do it on a plain click with "Open apps on this dock's screen"
new: Space Awareness app order: Sort each Space's apps by name, by launch order, or in your own Custom order. In Dock Edit Mode you can drag apps to reorder them in Space Awareness, Live Dock and Running Apps, or use Move Left / Right / Start / End from the right-click menu
new: Minimize / Restore All Windows: A new Space Awareness left-click choice that tucks away or brings back all of an app's windows at once
new: Hidden and minimized apps stay put: Space Awareness keeps them on their own Space's strip, shown dimmed
new: Show apps with no windows: A new Space Awareness setting (off by default) shows apps that are running with no open windows, dimmed on every Space. Thanks to Sander for asking
new: Hide an app from Space Awareness: Right-click an app on a Space Awareness strip and choose "Hide from Space Awareness". Before, the menu offered "Remove from Dock" there, which did nothing. Thanks to Sander for the report
new: Move the macOS Dock to the side: Mission Control always shows the macOS Dock, so this new Deep-hide option moves it to a side edge and makes it small. When one of your docks shares an edge with the macOS Dock, Settings → General offers it with a one-click "Move to the Side". Turn it off and your Dock comes back as it was
new: File Shelf Quick Look: With the File Shelf open, press Space to preview the ticked files or the file under the pointer. Arrow keys step through several files, and right-click → Quick Look works too
improved: Clear help when a license is needed: Without an active license your docks stay on screen, and using one opens a small "Activate ExtraDock" window with Open License Settings and Get a License buttons. Settings, the Manager and the menu bar keep working
improved: Deep-hide stays hidden: If the macOS Dock comes back after a restart or logout, ExtraDock hides it again
improved: Badges from the start: New docks show notification badges (Slack, Teams, Mail…) right away. Existing docks keep their setting
improved: Smoother window restore: Bringing back a minimized window no longer freezes the dock while it animates
improved: File Shelf keeps your app in front: Opening the File Shelf no longer brings ExtraDock forward, so anything you type still goes to the app you were using
improved: Cleaner menus: The menu bar menu has shorter names ("ExtraDock Manager", "Settings", "Export Logs", "Check for Updates"), New Dock sits with your docks, and each dock opens straight in the Manager. The + menu has Items, Widgets, Monitoring Dock and Add Tab in one place
improved: Clearer styles: The Style editor explains when Reduce Transparency or "Always use opaque dock backgrounds" turns Blur or Liquid Glass into a solid color
improved: Preview exclusions: Pick excluded apps with an app picker. They stay excluded after a language change or when an app's name differs
improved: Better problem reports: Export Logs includes more dock details and full crash reports, so we can fix things faster
improved: Groundwork for upcoming translations (English text unchanged)
bug fix: Menus keep the dock open: A hover-expanded notch dock or an auto-hide dock no longer closes while one of its right-click menus is open
bug fix: Settings links open the right page: Open License Settings and the other buttons and links that send you to Settings now land on the right page
bug fix: Network drives no longer slow the Trash: The Trash and System Trash widget no longer wait on network volumes that are slow or offline
bug fix: Smooth Space switching: Space Awareness no longer briefly resizes when you switch Spaces
bug fix: Docks on the bottom edge: With "Move the macOS Dock to the side" on, docks on the bottom edge sit flush instead of floating above an empty strip
bug fix: Finder widget dot: Its running dot now matches the dots under your other apps
bug fix: Edit Tab: The color swatch lines up with its label
