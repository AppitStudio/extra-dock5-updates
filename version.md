VERSION: 5.2.7
DETAILS:

new: Show Windows Separately: Right-click an app on your dock (or open Manager › Items) and turn on "Show Windows Separately". Each open window of that app gets its own dock item right after it, in the order you opened them (up to 10, including windows on other Spaces). Click one to bring that exact window to the front. Minimized windows show dimmed, and you can right-click a window item to close it (the app still asks you to save). Works in Native Dock Copy and Running Apps sections too. It's off by default and set per app
new: Search in Settings: A search field now sits at the top of the Settings sidebar. Type what you're looking for in your own words ("hide dock", "make the icons bigger"), pick a result, and Settings opens the right page, scrolls to the setting and highlights it
new: Liquid Glass tone: Under Style › Liquid Glass, pick Clear, Light, Dark or Automatic. Dark is new, and Automatic follows macOS Light and Dark mode live. "Dark glass" is now called Clear and looks the same. Your docks keep their look, and new docks start on Automatic
new: Docks go back when a Focus ends: Your ExtraDock Focus filter already shows or hides docks when a Focus turns on. Turn on Restore Docks When Focus Ends in Settings › Behavior › Focus Filters and, when the Focus turns off, those docks go back the way they were. A dock you changed by hand during the Focus stays the way you left it. Off by default
new: Drag a file onto a running app: Rest a dragged file on a running app's dock icon and, after a moment, the app comes forward with its windows so you can drop the file right where you want it. It follows the macOS Spring-loading setting
new: Clock date color: The date on the Clock widget now follows your Text color, so it stays easy to read on a dark wallpaper. A new Date color setting lets you pick a different color for the date
new: Bluetooth charging: The Bluetooth Battery widget shows a green bolt while a Magic Keyboard, Mouse or Trackpad is charging
improved: Settings opens pages instantly: Every Settings page shows at once, and changing a setting no longer makes your docks and widgets redraw. Closing Settings gives the memory back
improved: Smoother docks: Adding or removing apps, window items and running apps is lighter, and the dock never shifts while it animates
improved: Quicker launch: The Launch at Login and DockFlow connection checks no longer hold up ExtraDock at launch or while you work
improved: TamaDocky goes easy on your Mac: An idle pet uses far less CPU, and it pauses while it's covered, the screen is locked or the display sleeps
improved: Widgets stay put while you change a dock: Resizing or renaming a dock no longer restarts widgets such as the File Shelf and the System Trash, so they keep showing what they showed
improved: Dock tabs make room for previews: With dock tabs set to "On hover", the tab strip steps aside while a window preview is open, so it never covers the preview
improved: One icon per copy: Apps that run more than one copy at a time (like Wormhole) now show a dock icon for each copy, with its window title
improved: Trash empties right away: After you empty items you deleted from iCloud Drive, the Trash on your dock shows empty within seconds, even without Full Disk Access
improved: GIF widget follows your file: The GIF widget plays the file you chose and picks up your edits to it. If you added a GIF widget in 5.2.6, choose its GIF once more so it can follow the file
bug fix: TamaDocky stays awake: A TamaDocky pet on a dock that collapses and expands no longer turns gray and stops responding after a few minutes
bug fix: Windows open on the desktop you're on: With several desktops, Settings, the Manager, the activation window and widget windows such as File Shelf and Trash open on the desktop in use instead of pulling you to another one
bug fix: Shift-click stays on your desktop: Shift-clicking an app to bring it to this screen never switches you to another desktop. If the app's window is on a desktop you're not looking at, ExtraDock beeps and leaves you where you are
bug fix: Reserve Dock Space and dragged windows: With Active on Drag off, a window you're holding with the mouse is no longer moved mid-drag. It's moved out of the dock area after you let go
bug fix: System Trash widget: It keeps its icon while you drag the Icon Size slider, "Allow Finder Access…" only appears while access is missing, and Widget Settings… and Remove Widget are back in its menu
bug fix: Settings window buttons: The close, minimize and zoom buttons no longer crowd the top-left corner on macOS Tahoe
